## 1. The s1/s2 junction
The runner is `scripts/run_injection.py` (sha256 1aadb39d…, unchanged), in one-stage mode. v2.3 used the same runner
through `scripts/run_onset.py` (f04b3200…).

**The prompt.** It is the full rendered string, sent as text to `/v1/completions` with `skip_special_tokens=false`. For
all 1,500 C1 records and all 1,200 v2.3 records it starts with `<bos>` and ends with `<|channel>thought\n`: the July
template forces an open thought after a tool response, with thinking on.
- `s1_text` therefore starts **inside** the thought. It is the generated text up to, but excluding, the first
  `<|tool_call>`.
- Between `s1_text` and `s2_text` sits exactly the token `<|tool_call>` (id 48). The **model generated it** inside the same
  request; the runner did not force it.
- `s2_text` is everything after it, up to the stop token `<tool_call|>` (id 49). That token is excluded from the text,
  and the call counts as closed iff the request stopped on 49.
- The full emission is therefore `prompt + s1_text + "<|tool_call>" + s2_text + "<tool_call|>"`.

**Exception: budget-forced records.** These are records whose thought exceeded 4,096 tokens: C1 P8a 1/300, P0 0, P1 0;
v2.3 O0 3/600, O1 5/600; flag `budget_forced`.
- The runner cut the thought at 4,096 tokens and **inserted `<channel|>` itself**. It then regenerated with seed + 1,
  stopping at `<|tool_call>` (id 48, generated by the model), re-appended `<|tool_call>`, and finished the call with seed + 2,
  stopping at 49.
- Their `s1_text` contains that runner-inserted `<channel|>`, and `s2_stop` = 49.
